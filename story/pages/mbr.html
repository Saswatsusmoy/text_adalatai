Minimum Bayes Risk decoding is an inference-only ablation on top of the shipped A2 adapters. No retrain, no reward model, no new dependencies. Sample N candidates per source, output the one whose mean pairwise sentence-chrF++ vs peers is highest (Eikema & Aziz 2020; Freitag et al. 2022, chrF utility).

Real runs (A2 adapters)

Two runs on identical data + adapters + sacreBLEU signatures, decode differs. MPS beam4 control isolates the confound between H200 bf16 shipped and MPS fp16 laptop run.

System I_test (n=190) E_milpac_test (n=117)
A2 beam4 (H200 bf16, shipped) 21.86 / 49.66 34.90 / 56.46
A2 beam4 (MPS fp16, control) 21.85 / 49.68 34.71 / 56.55
A2 MBR N=8 top_p=0.9 T=1.0 (MPS fp16) 18.16 / 47.13 31.39 / 54.07

Device/precision delta (MPS beam4 − H200 beam4): I_test −0.01 / +0.02, E_milpac −0.18 / +0.09 — inside noise.

Decode-only delta (MPS MBR − MPS beam4): I_test −3.68 BLEU / −2.56 chrF++, E_milpac −3.33 BLEU / −2.48 chrF++.

E_anuvaad_test (n=3000) not run: ~4h on MPS at this rate.

Verdict

At N=8, top_p=0.9, T=1.0, chrF++ utility, MBR does not help. Beam4 stays shipped. The confound is decode strategy, not device or precision. Negative result stands only at the tried configuration. Phase 4 caveat: these deltas are point estimates (the MBR-vs-beam4 differences were not run through a paired CI); the two systems share the same source set, so a paired difference CI is the right follow-up tool.

Follow-ups not run

  • Lower temperature (T=0.3–0.5) or epsilon-sampling (Freitag 2022) — current top_p=0.9 T=1.0 is high-diversity and dilutes MBR consensus.
  • Larger N (32–128) — MBR literature typically uses N >= 32; N=8 is the low end.
  • COMET / CometKiwi utility — reference-free QE-based MBR is where recent papers report the wins.
  • E_anuvaad_test full — needs H200 or overnight MPS budget.
