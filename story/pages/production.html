Why A2

  • Best joint dual-policy score across I_test, E_milpac, E_anuvaad.
  • Clear gains vs zero-shot without external collapse.
  • LoRA-only adapter is small (~tens of MB) and attachable at inference.
  • Curriculum A1 -> A2 is reproducible from configs and Makefile.
  • Chosen by the z-scored, cap-enforced selection harness: Stage B was rejected because its E_milpac drop exceeded the anti-forget bar (see Track D).

Why not the other candidates (in the selection harness's terms)

Every alternative was measured against the same dual-policy floor. The rejected systems all fail on the external axis or the quality axis:

The Phase 3 harness makes the Stage B rejection automatic: a checkpoint whose capped suite drops past its bar can no longer become best_primary. The historical pure-B run was only caught by post-hoc analysis; a re-run today would be rejected in-loop.

How confident are these numbers? (Phase 4)

The scoreboard was produced before the Phase 4 eval harness, so the deltas above are point estimates (no confidence intervals). Going forward every report carries per-suite 95% bootstrap CIs and a paired-bootstrap difference CI, so "A2 vs B'" and "A2 vs DoRA" can be stated with intervals and a significant flag. See Dual eval and Scoreboard.

Inference recipe

# attach the A2 LoRA adapters to stock NLLB and decode
PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m src.evaluation.zero_shot_nllb \
  --adapters data/runs/nllb600_A_A2_h200_A2_ddp2_20260726T212958Z/checkpoints/best_primary \
  --tag A2_best \
  --device cuda   # or mps / cpu
# --score-only with --max-pairs honors the report's n; --compare-tags A,B gives
# a paired difference CI between two systems' hyp files

Adapters are PEFT folders; base weights come from HuggingFace facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M. The run is registered in data/runs/runs.json so its config snapshot, data manifest, and eval logs are one lookup away.
