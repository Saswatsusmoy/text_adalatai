Automatic metrics understate legal register. These Policy I examples compare zero-shot NLLB to A2 LoRA on terms, party roles, and formal phrasing. References can be OCR-truncated; examples focus on relative improvement. Full hyps: data/analysis/zero_shot_nllb_I_test_hyps.jsonl, nllb600_A2_h200_best_I_test_hyps.jsonl.

Phase 4 makes part of this quantitative: the entity panel now reports per-suite legal-entity recall/precision/F1 (probes + case citations + dates, matched across scripts), so a term like Article 227 / अनुच्छेद 227 is counted, not just eyeballed. See Dual eval.

1. Legal term -- revisional court / Article 227

EN

Assailing the order of the revisional court, the appellant filed a petition under Article 227 of the Constitution of India, invoking the supervisory jurisdiction of the High Court of Judicature at Allahabad.

Reference (partial)

पुनरीक्षण अदालत के आदेश को चुनौती देते हुए, अपीलकर्ता ने भारत के संविधान के अनुच्छेद 227 के तहत ...

Zero-shot

पुनरावलोकन अदालत के आदेश पर बल देते हुए ... (uses "review" sense, wrong legal term)

A2 LoRA

पुनरीक्षण न्यायालय के आदेश पर बल देते हुए, अपीलकर्ता ने भारत के संविधान के अनुच्छेद 227 के तहत एक याचिका दायर की ...

A2 recovers domain term पुनरीक्षण (revisional) over zero-shot पुनरावलोकन.

2. Constitutional jurisdiction -- Article 142

EN

We also direct, in exercise of our jurisdiction under Article 142 of the Constitution of India, that his salary should be paid over for the period for which he works until a regular candidate is appointed.

Zero-shot

... अनुच्छेद 142 ... अधिकार क्षेत्र ...

A2 LoRA

... अनुच्छेद 142 ... अधिकारिता ... (more formal legal register for jurisdiction)

Register shifts toward formal legal Hindi after domain LoRA.

3. Writ petition compound

EN

7 The writ petition filed by the appellant was dismissed by a learned Single Judge of the High Court on 9 October 2013.

Zero-shot

... दायर याचिका ... (drops "writ")

A2 LoRA

... दायर रिट याचिका ...

Compound legal noun रिट याचिका preserved after FT.

4. Party role -- appellant vs petitioner

EN

dismissing the Writ Petition filed by the Appellant.

Zero-shot

याचिकाकर्ता द्वारा दायर लिखित याचिका ... (petitioner -- wrong role)

A2 LoRA

अपीलकर्ता द्वारा दायर लिखित याचिका ...

Party terminology fidelity matters in judgments.

5. Archaic / formal legal English

EN

It was also observed by the revisional court that although it was averred by the appellant that he was put in dark by the counsel earlier engaged by him, there is no reference to his name.

Zero-shot

पुनरावलोकन ... English leak "appellant" ... वकील

A2 LoRA

पुनरीक्षण ... अपीलकर्ता ... अधिवक्ता

Less English leak; advocate register over colloquial counsel.

Residual issues (honest)

  • "Impugned order" often remains bare आदेश rather than आक्षेपित आदेश in both systems.
  • OCR references can be truncated or year-noisy; automatic scores mix model and ref error.
  • Long multi-clause sentences still need legal QA for production use.
