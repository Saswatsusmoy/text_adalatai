Selection and anti-forget

During training, checkpoints are ranked with a weighted primary score on dev suites (chrF++). Since the Phase 3 harness fix, Stage B uses a z-scored primary (weights mean what they say across differently-scaled suites) plus hard caps enforced in the loop (see Track D):

Stage B anti-forget bar (now enforced by the trainer)

E_milpac chrF++ drop must be <= 2.0 vs A2. Pure Stage B dropped ~5.24 -- fail. B' with 10% A2 replay recovered most of E but still did not beat A2 dual-policy. A checkpoint that violates the cap can no longer become best_primary; it is rejected and counts toward early-stop patience.
