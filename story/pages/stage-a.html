The assignment corpus yields only ~1.5k clean pairs after alignment. That is enough for evaluation and light specialization, but not enough for serious domain adaptation. Stage A adds public legal EN-HI bitext at scale, then carves dual-policy holdouts so train never touches the external tests.

Sources

SourceLicenseRole
MILPaC (Law-AI)CC BY-NC-SA 4.0Clean legal EN-HI pairs
Anuvaad legal EN-HICC BY 4.0Scale: judiciary, HC/SUVAS, law commission, terms, names, augmented

Both sources are already aligned upstream. We do not re-OCR or re-run LaBSE on the full pool (cost would dominate with little gain). Schema normalize + shared length QC only.

Ingest pipeline

make external-download   # optional fetch into data/external/raw/
make external-ingest     # -> data/external/parallel/*.jsonl
make external-eval-split # Policy E holdouts + stage_a_train.jsonl

Module: src/preprocessing/ingest_external_parallel.py.

Filters (aligned with assignment QC philosophy): EN:HI char ratio 0.3-3.0, min length > 3, exact (en, hi) pair dedup. No LaBSE re-score.
