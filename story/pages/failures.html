What I learned from failures

  • Measure extraction quality before building NLP steps on garbage text layers.
  • Hold out external domain data before celebrating assignment-test gains.
  • Tokenizer packing and MT quality are different objectives; do not conflate them.
  • Checkpoint what you actually train (embedding rows outside PEFT adapters).
  • Make data-quality invariants a code-enforced gate, and keep tests from writing into the real data tree.
