Likely questions and short answers

Why not only optimize the tokenizer?

Packing reduces cost, but legal fidelity needs model adaptation. We measured both. Production prioritizes quality with stock NLLB tokens after vocab-extend lost dual-policy scores.

Why NLLB instead of TinyLLaMA / Mistral?

Sentence-level legal translation is encoder-decoder MT territory. LoRA on NLLB is sample-efficient and single-GPU friendly; LLMs need more scaffolding for this task.

Why is Stage B not production?

I BLEU rose but external legal chrF collapsed (MILPaC drop ~5.2). Dual policy exists exactly to catch that. Prefer A2 or a replay Stage B' if specializing further.

Was vocab extend a waste?

No. Controlled negative result (bulk vs careful, emb checkpoint bug found). Prevents shipping a clever tokenizer story without quality.

Is this still "prototype" depth?

Core path is preprocess + tokenizer benches + LoRA smoke + dual metrics -- Colab-class. Dual-track full tables are extra rigor on optional hardware.
