How to read the board

  • chrF++ is the primary quality metric for Hindi (character n-grams + word unigrams).
  • BLEU is reported for completeness; alone it under-represents HI morphology.
  • I vs E: I is the assignment stress test; E catches overfit to 30 docs.
  • Stage B is the classic trap: better I BLEU, worse dual policy.
  • C1c shows tokenizer surgery cost: method improvements (v2) still lost quality on A1 budget.
