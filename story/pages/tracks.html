Why dual-track

  • Track D answers: "What is the best practical adapter for legal EN-HI?"
  • Track C answers: "Does domain tokenization improve packing enough to justify model surgery?"

Both are assignment-relevant. Production chooses quality. Science documents packing and the negative transfer of careless vocab extend.

Execution order (actual)

  1. Freeze data contract (doc IDs, dual policies).
  2. C0: train/freeze joint_full_41000 SPM.
  3. D: zero-shot NLLB on I + E.
  4. D: Stage A1 then A2 LoRA.
  5. D: Stage B (+ later B' replay) anti-forget check.
  6. C1c: bulk v1 ablation + careful v2 + full I+E.
  7. Head-to-head table; production = D A2.
  8. Report + this walkthrough.
