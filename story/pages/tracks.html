Why dual-track

  • Track D answers: "What is the best practical adapter for legal EN-HI?"
  • Track C answers: "Does domain tokenization improve packing enough to justify model surgery?"

Both are assignment-relevant. Production chooses quality. Science documents packing and the negative transfer of careless vocab extend.

Why NLLB + LoRA, not a small LLM

  • Task shape: sentence-level legal MT is encoder-decoder territory; an LLM needs extra scaffolding (prompting, decoding, licensing) for the same job.
  • Efficiency: LoRA on NLLB-600M is ~0.5% trainable params, single-GPU / MPS friendly, and resumes cleanly between curriculum stages.
  • Prior quality: NLLB's multilingual pretraining already gives strong EN-HI; adaptation is a small correction, not a rebuild.
  • Dual-track fairness: both tracks share frozen assignment splits + the same dual-policy tests, so the head-to-head is apples-to-apples.

Execution order (actual)

  1. Freeze data contract (doc IDs, dual policies).
  2. C0: train/freeze joint_full_41000 SPM.
  3. D: zero-shot NLLB on I + E.
  4. D: Stage A1 then A2 LoRA.
  5. D: Stage B (+ later B' replay) anti-forget check.
