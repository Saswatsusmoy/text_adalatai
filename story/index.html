Two pillars (assignment objective)

  1. Token efficiency for Indic (Hindi / Devanagari) under modern tokenizers and LLMs.
  2. Small-scale domain adaptation for high-fidelity legal translation (not full-scale training).
Bottom line

Ship Track D A2 LoRA. Reject Stage B (external anti-forget fail). Reject C1c vocab-extend (below zero-shot on full dual-policy tests). Domain SPM science stands as packing evidence; production MT keeps the NLLB native tokenizer.
